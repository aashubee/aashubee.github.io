About Me

Hello there! My name is Aashrith Beesabathuni, a recent EECS graduate from UC Berkeley, Class of 2026, with a focus on hardware. My area of interest is robotics, as I love the intersection between hardware and software to create a system for any purpose. I believe the best way to learn is by doing, so I love gaining hands-on experience to learn new skills!I wanted to create a portfolio to show off some of my technical work throughout the years, from class final projects, to personal PCBs I have made to hone my skills. On the non-technical end, I love photography, and expressing my creativity through street and concert style photos. Feel free to reach out if you want to chat about anything that interests you on here!
